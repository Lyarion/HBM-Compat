Use in HBM Compat:
  HBM Compat does NOT copy or redistribute any of HBM's fluid textures.
  For each HBM fluid it registers as a Forge fluid, the block-atlas sprite
  (io.github.hbmcompat.client.HbmFluidSprite) reads HBM's own swatch PNG
  directly from the HBM jar through the resource manager at texture-stitch
  time, applying HBM's per-fluid guiTint exactly as HBM does. Because NTM is
  a hard runtime dependency of this mod, its assets are always present; no
  art is bundled here. When HBM ships no dedicated swatch for a fluid (e.g.
  custom/foreign fluids that reuse a shared *_base texture), a solid swatch
  is synthesized from the fluid's own colour instead.

  This runtime-reuse approach means the textures remain served solely from
  the HBM jar under HBM's own license; this mod ships none of them.
